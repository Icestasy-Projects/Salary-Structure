# Job Description: Product & Operations Manager

**Company:** Icestasy Projects. B2B ice cream ingredients and premixes for commercial customers and distributors
**Function:** Product Management & Business Operations
**Reports to:** Founder / Managing Director
**Location:** India

---

## Role Summary

The Product & Operations Manager finds operational problems in the business and turns them into working internal products that people actually use. The role takes a process from a manual, error-prone state to a standard, repeatable one, and owns the full cycle: requirements, business rules, build, testing, rollout and ongoing improvement.

The first product delivered in this role was the **Employee Salary Structure Calculator**. It is an internal tool that turns an annual CTC figure into a complete, compliant monthly salary breakdown and produces a branded, printable salary structure document.

---

## Work Delivered to Date

### 1. Product Discovery & Problem Definition
- Found the need for a standard salary-structuring process to replace manual, spreadsheet-based CTC breakups that varied from one offer to the next.
- Set the product scope: a single input (Annual CTC) that produces a complete, explainable output for HR, finance and candidates.
- Defined the primary users (founders, HR/admin, finance) and the main output (a one-page salary structure document suitable for offers and internal approval).

### 2. Business Rules & Compensation Design
- Translated the company's compensation policy into structured, rule-based logic:
  - **CTC split:** 75% fixed / 25% variable pay.
  - **Salary components:** Basic (50% of fixed), HRA (50% of Basic), LTA (10% of Basic), with the remaining balance as a variable allowance.
  - **Monthly gross** derived after removing variable pay and employer statutory costs from CTC.
- Designed the structure so that the employer's total cost stays within the agreed CTC, which prevents cost overruns at the offer stage.

### 3. Statutory & Payroll Compliance Logic
- Built Indian statutory contributions and deductions into the product:
  - **Provident Fund (PF):** employer and employee contributions at 12%, capped at the statutory wage ceiling (₹1,800/month).
  - **ESIC:** employer (3.25%) and employee (0.75%) contributions, applied only below the ₹21,000 eligibility threshold.
  - **EPF Admin Charges and EDLI.**
  - **Labour Welfare Fund (LWF)** and **Professional Tax (PT).**
- Showed employer contributions and employee deductions as separate items, so that both the cost to the company and the employee's take-home pay are clear.

### 4. Product Build & Delivery
- Delivered a lightweight, browser-based tool that needs no installation, no login and no ongoing software cost.
- Real-time calculation: every figure updates as the CTC is entered.
- Output features: **Print** and **one-click PDF download**, with the layout tuned to fit cleanly on a single A4 page.
- Indian currency formatting (₹, en-IN digit grouping) throughout.

### 5. Brand & Presentation
- Applied company branding (logo, colour system, document layout) so that every salary structure goes out as a professional, consistent company document.
- Revised the logo placement, sizing and PDF density over several rounds, based on how the printed output looked.

### 6. Iterative Delivery & Version Control
- Managed the product through structured, version-controlled releases (multiple reviewed and merged change sets), each one fixing a specific usability or output problem.
- Ran short feedback cycles: build → review output → refine → release.

## Core Responsibilities (Ongoing)

### Product Management
- Find high-friction, manual or error-prone processes across HR, finance, sales, procurement and production, and rank them by business impact.
- Write clear requirements and business rules before anything is built.
- Own the product roadmap for internal tools and digital workflows.
- Define success metrics (time saved, errors removed, adoption) and track them after launch.
- Keep each product accurate as policy, statutory rates or business conditions change.

### Operations
- Document and standardise core business processes (SOPs) across functions.
- Support the HR and payroll operations frameworks: compensation structuring, offer documentation and statutory compliance checks.
- Work with finance on cost visibility: cost-to-company, unit costs and margin reporting.
- Improve how operations are run day to day by removing bottlenecks, cutting handoffs and making information easier to find.
- Act as the link between leadership intent and day-to-day execution.

### Cross-Functional Coordination
- Work with founders, HR, finance, sales and production to collect requirements and test solutions.
- Train users on new tools and processes, and collect feedback for the next iteration.
- Manage outside vendors or tools where a build is not the right answer.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Business, Engineering, Commerce or a related field. An MBA or operations/product certification is a plus.
- 2–5 years in product management, business operations, or a hybrid ops/product role, ideally in manufacturing, FMCG or food & beverage.
- Working knowledge of Indian payroll and statutory compliance (PF, ESIC, PT, LWF).
- Comfortable with spreadsheets, no-code or low-code tools, and basic web technologies.
- Clear written communication: can turn a policy into rules and a process into an SOP.
